Questions

Good to know

Do you speak English?

Yes. You can book online in English, or write on WhatsApp – I reply in English.

How do I book?

Choose a service and duration above and tap “Book”. The online booking (Booqme) is in English and shows free times. You can also write on WhatsApp.

Where are you?

Hodžova 4304/20, Piešťany – a private studio about 10 minutes’ walk from the spa island. Open daily 8:00 – 20:00.

How do I pay?

Cash or card at the studio, after the treatment.

Is the massage suitable during a spa stay?

Relaxation massage, cupping and wood therapy complement spa treatments well. If you have a medical condition or are under treatment, please check with your doctor first.

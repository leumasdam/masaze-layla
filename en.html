Good to know

Massage in Piešťany: questions & answers.

Do you speak English?

Yes. The online booking is in English, and you can write on WhatsApp – I reply in English.

How do I book?

Choose a treatment and duration above and tap “Book”. The booking opens with that service selected and shows the free times. You can also write on WhatsApp or call.

Where is the studio?

Hodžova 4304/20, Piešťany – a private studio in the town centre, about 10 minutes’ walk from the spa island. Open every day 8:00 – 20:00.

How do I pay?

Cash or card at the studio, after the treatment.

Is massage suitable during a spa stay?

Relaxation massage, cupping and wood therapy go well with a spa stay. If you have a medical condition or are under treatment, please check with your doctor first.

Relaxation massage

What is a relaxation massage?

A relaxation massage is a calm massage with oil, working step by step over the back, neck, shoulders, arms and legs. I adapt the pressure to you: some want a gentle, soothing touch, others need stiff areas worked more thoroughly.

If one spot bothers you, choose a targeted back, neck and shoulder, head and face or foot massage. For a sensitive or sore back I use soft techniques that relax the muscles gently, without strong pressure.

Questions

Massage: questions & answers.

How much is a massage in Piešťany?

At Masáže Layla a full-body relaxation massage is 20 € (30 minutes), 30 € (60 minutes) and 50 € (90 minutes). Focused back, head or foot massages are 20 € for 30 minutes and 30 € for 60 minutes.

Which length should I choose?

In 30 minutes I can work on one area, most often the back and neck. 60 minutes is enough for the whole body or a thorough back massage. 90 minutes is a full-body massage without any rush.

How do I book?

The quickest way is online through the booking system, where you see free times straight away. You can also call +421 904 098 246 or write on Instagram @masazelayla.

Where the studio is and when it is open

Questions

Massage in Piešťany: questions & answers.

Everything you need to know before your massage – clearly, in one place.

Which massages do you offer in Piešťany?

Full-body relaxation massage, back, neck and shoulder massage, head and face massage, foot massage, soft tissue techniques, cupping and wood therapy with lymphatic techniques.

How much is a massage?

A 30-minute massage is 20 €, 60 minutes 30 € and a 90-minute full-body relaxation massage 50 €. Wood therapy is 40 € for 60 minutes and 60 € for 90 minutes.

How do I book?

Online through the booking system, where you see free times, by phone on +421 904 098 246 or with a message on Instagram @masazelayla.

Where are you and when are you open?

The Masáže Layla studio is at Hodžova 4304/20 in Piešťany. You can book any day of the week from 8:00 to 20:00.

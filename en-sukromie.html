Privacy & cookies

This page explains what data the website masazelayla.sk processes and how to set cookies. Valid from 1 October 2026.

Who processes the data

The operator is Masáže Layla, Hodžova 4304/20, 921 01 Piešťany. Contact: masazelayla@gmail.com, +421 904 098 246.

What data I process

  • Contact form message: your name, contact and message text you send me by e-mail. I use them only to reply.
  • Booking: takes place in the Booqme booking system. Booqme processes the data you enter there under its own terms; I see it to the extent needed to arrange your appointment.
  • News by e-mail: if you leave your e-mail on the website and tick consent, I send you a discount code and occasionally free slots and offers. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying or via the link in the e-mail.
  • Visitor and advertising measurement: only if you agree in the cookie settings.

Cookies

  • Necessary: the site remembers your cookie choice. No consent needed, always active.
  • Analytics (Google Analytics): anonymous statistics on how many people visited the site and which pages they read. Only with consent.
  • Marketing (Google Ads): measuring whether an ad brought a visitor, and showing ads to people who have visited the site. Only with consent.

Your rights

You have the right to know what data I hold about you, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and to withdraw consent at any time. Just write to masazelayla@gmail.com. If you believe I am not handling your data correctly, you can contact the Slovak Office for Personal Data Protection.
